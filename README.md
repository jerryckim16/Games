# Games
I make games here through vibecoding on Claude

## Games
- [Lotto 6/45 Simulator](lotto645/) — play Korea's Lotto 6/45 every week until you win the jackpot. Choose how many tickets to buy per draw and watch your net profit, money spent, and years pass on a live chart. Open `lotto645/index.html` in a browser (no build step).
- [Warehouse Robot Simulator](robotsim/) — 1–20 robots shuttle items from 1–100 pickup stations to 1–100 drop-off spots on a grid warehouse that grows to fit, with optional battery charging stations (0–20) robots must visit before they run flat, while the sim counts collisions (same-cell and head-on swaps). Ships with collision-blind shortest path (A*) and four collision-avoiding planners: Cooperative A* (replans every tick), Reservation A* (plans each trip once, first-come station claims), Windowed CBS (Conflict-Based Search plans all robots jointly a few ticks ahead) and PIBT (one-step priority inheritance; very fast and strongest when many robots share few stations). Pathfinding algorithms are pluggable: add one to `robotsim/planners.js` and it appears in the dropdown, then use the Benchmark panel to compare algorithms on the same seed. Open `robotsim/index.html` in a browser (no build step). Details, algorithm descriptions and benchmark results: [robotsim/README.md](robotsim/README.md).
- [Suneung Simulator](suneung/) — a life-sim of the Korean college entrance exam (수능). Pick boy or girl, then you're born at random (sometimes to a single parent, to grandparents or into a children's home that you leave at 18 with the real state settlement, with parents who each have a real job, from doctor or civil servant to shop owner or driver, dealt to fit the family's income and changing with layoffs and life, and parents can divorce or die along the way; with a temperament and a 돌잡이 grab at your first birthday that give you permanent bonus traits, and a fortune-teller's reading of how lucky a life you've been dealt) into one of Korea's five household-income fifths and one of seventeen real places (from Gangnam and Mokdong to Jeju and rural Haenam), which shape cram schools, daily life and admission quotas and show up as a pin on an interactive map of Korea drawn from its real province and district borders; you can move house by picking a place on that map, pick your school at each stage (public or private, or 자사고, science, foreign language, arts or vocational high schools, where the employment office may offer you a job in High 3 instead of the 수능), each one a real school from the national list, shown on a close-up map of your area, play the baby and kindergarten years, start elementary school at 6 (and leave school from Middle 1 to study for the GED and the 수능 on your own), and grow up turn by turn, with school lessons raising every subject a little on their own (a year, a semester, a season, then month by month in High 3, 고3): which subjects to study, whether your family can pay for cram school or a tutor, when to read, rest, play or see friends, while luck swings everything (family fortunes that rise and fall, accidents, lucky breaks, a good or bad 수능 day, surprise cuts and waitlist calls) and random events test your stress, health and motivation, and bullying can drag on until you deal with it; named classmates, a best friend, a rival, crushes and a new homeroom teacher every year come and go with you, and the ending shows where your friends ended up. Pick humanities or sciences at the end of High 1 (고1), sit mock exams and then the 수능 itself, simulated question by question against a nationwide cohort and graded with real 표준점수, 백분위 and 등급. In high school every midterm and final counts toward your school grades (내신, the real 5-grade scale, graded against your classmates, so selective schools are harder), and clubs, volunteering, research projects and teachers' comments build your school record (학생부). In September of High 3 send up to six early applications (수시) on your grades, your record (with interviews) or an essay exam, with real minimum 수능 grades, and any early offer rules out regular admission; otherwise apply to up to three universities in 정시 (40 schools, including the women's universities, KAIST and POSTECH), then enroll or retake as many years as it takes.. Then live the next ten years: a major, university life, military service for men, the job hunt (big companies, public enterprises, the civil service exam, law school, residency) and where you are at 30, with your pay ranked against the family you were born into. Achievements and a list of past lives (each one replayable) carry across lives, today's life gives everyone the same start on a given date, and each life ends with a share card you can save as a PNG. Everything is illustrated with original inline-SVG art: a scene for each stage of life, an avatar that grows up and shows its mood, and a picture for every event, dark and sad when something bad happens and warm when it goes well, and short animated cutscenes for the milestones (birth, the 돌잡이, school days, 수능 morning, results, admission). Play in English or Korean. Open `suneung/index.html` in a browser (no build step). Details, exam model and balance numbers: [suneung/README.md](suneung/README.md).
