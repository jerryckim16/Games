# Games
I make games here through vibecoding on Claude

## Games
- [Lotto 6/45 Simulator](lotto645/) — play Korea's Lotto 6/45 every week until you win the jackpot. Choose how many tickets to buy per draw and watch your net profit, money spent, and years pass on a live chart. Open `lotto645/index.html` in a browser (no build step).
- [Warehouse Robot Simulator](robotsim/) — 1–20 robots shuttle items from 1–100 pickup stations to 1–100 drop-off spots on a grid warehouse that grows to fit, with optional battery charging stations (0–20) robots must visit before they run flat, while the sim counts collisions (same-cell and head-on swaps). Ships with collision-blind shortest path (A*) and four collision-avoiding planners: Cooperative A* (replans every tick), Reservation A* (plans each trip once, first-come station claims), Windowed CBS (Conflict-Based Search plans all robots jointly a few ticks ahead) and PIBT (one-step priority inheritance; very fast and strongest when many robots share few stations). Pathfinding algorithms are pluggable: add one to `robotsim/planners.js` and it appears in the dropdown, then use the Benchmark panel to compare algorithms on the same seed. Open `robotsim/index.html` in a browser (no build step). Details, algorithm descriptions and benchmark results: [robotsim/README.md](robotsim/README.md).
- [Suneung Simulator](suneung/) — a life-sim of the Korean college entrance exam (수능). Pick boy or girl, then you're born at random (with a temperament and a 돌잡이 grab at your first birthday that give you permanent bonus traits, and a fortune-teller's reading of how lucky a life you've been dealt) into one of Korea's five household-income fifths and one of seventeen real places (from Gangnam and Mokdong to Jeju and rural Haenam), which shape cram schools, daily life and admission quotas; you can move house, pick your school at each stage (public or private, or 자사고, science, foreign language, arts or vocational high schools), play the baby and kindergarten years, start elementary school at 6 (and leave school from Middle 1 to study for the GED and the 수능 on your own), and grow up turn by turn, with school lessons raising every subject a little on their own (a year, a semester, a season, then month by month in High 3, 고3): which subjects to study, whether your family can pay for cram school or a tutor, when to read, rest, play or see friends, while luck swings everything (family fortunes that rise and fall, accidents, lucky breaks, a good or bad 수능 day, surprise cuts and waitlist calls) and random events test your stress, health and motivation, and bullying can drag on until you deal with it. Pick humanities or sciences at the end of High 1 (고1), sit mock exams and then the 수능 itself, simulated question by question against a nationwide cohort and graded with real 표준점수, 백분위 and 등급. Apply to up to three universities, then enroll or retake as many years as it takes. Everything is illustrated with original inline-SVG art: a scene for each stage of life, an avatar that grows up and shows its mood, and a picture for every event. Play in English or Korean. Open `suneung/index.html` in a browser (no build step). Details, exam model and balance numbers: [suneung/README.md](suneung/README.md).
